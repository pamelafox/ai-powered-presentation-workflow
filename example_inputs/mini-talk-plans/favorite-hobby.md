# Mini talk plan: What I learned from a favorite hobby

## Audience

A general audience that may not share the hobby, but enjoys learning how people think through practice, mistakes, and improvement.

## Goal

By the end, the audience should understand one useful lesson from the hobby that also applies outside the hobby.

## Constraints

- 3 to 5 minutes
- 4 to 6 slides
- Include one concrete story
- Use simple speaker notes

## Draft outline

1. Introduce the hobby and why it matters to you
2. Share one specific moment of struggle or surprise
3. Explain the lesson you learned
4. Show how the lesson applies elsewhere
5. Final takeaway: the portable idea

## Speaker-note guidance

- Replace "favorite hobby" with a specific hobby before generating the deck.
- Use a real anecdote instead of a generic description.
- The theme can lean into the hobby visually, such as field notes, scorecards, recipe cards, sketchbook pages, or workshop labels.
